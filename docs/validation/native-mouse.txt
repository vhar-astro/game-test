Native Linux mouse validation
=============================

Environment: Godot 4.7.2, X11 driver forced on the local XWayland display, ctypes
libX11/libXtst bindings. The host matches one exact, uniquely titled probe window,
focuses that window, injects XTest events, then restores the original pointer/focus.

Foot controls:

    python3 tools/validate_mouse_host.py --project-root . --timeout 12

    NATIVE_LOOK_OK yaw=-0.36 pitch=0.135
    NATIVE_CLICK_OK attacks=1
    NATIVE_ESCAPE_OK mode=pause cursor=0
    NATIVE_RESUME_OK mode=explore cursor=2
    NATIVE_MOUSE_VALIDATION_OK

Ship controls:

    python3 tools/validate_mouse_host.py --project-root . --flight --artifact artifacts/mouse_probe_flight.json --timeout 15

    NATIVE_LOOK_OK controller=ship yaw=4.07859259796143 pitch=0.699
    NATIVE_CLICK_OK controller=ship cursor=2
    NATIVE_ESCAPE_OK mode=pause cursor=0
    NATIVE_RESUME_OK mode=flight cursor=2
    NATIVE_MOUSE_VALIDATION_OK

Causal HUD regression:

    python3 tools/validate_mouse_host.py --project-root . --screen-filter stop --expect-look-blocked --artifact artifacts/mouse_probe_stop.json --timeout 12

    CAUSAL_STOP yaw_before=0.0 yaw_after=0.0 pitch_after=0.0 delta=0.0
    CAUSAL_STOP_BLOCKED_OK

The STOP run reproduces the former full-screen HUD interception. The default probe
sets the gameplay HUD to pointer-transparent IGNORE and the two host runs above
observe real OS events reaching the foot and ship camera handlers.
